Wait a few seconds after Send, so you can take the message back. Undo Send holds a message after you click Send. A small window counts down. Choose Undo send before it reaches zero and the message stays in the compose window. Key features:
  • The wait is between 5 and 30 seconds. The default is 10.
  • Undo keeps the message as a draft in the compose window.
  • Turn the delay off and messages go out as soon as you click Send.
Installation:
  1. In Thunderbird, open the menu and choose Add-ons and Themes.
  2. Click the gear icon and choose Install Add-on From File…, then select the .xpi. Or type Undo Send in the search field, open this add-on, and choose Add to Thunderbird.
  3. When Thunderbird asks for access, choose Add.
This add-on needs Thunderbird 128 or newer. The delay is on as soon as the add-on is installed. Send a message Write the message and click Send. A window titled Undo send opens and shows how many seconds are left.
  • Choose Undo send to stop the message. It stays in the compose window so you can edit it and send it again.
  • Wait for the countdown to finish and the message goes out on its own.
  • Closing the countdown window does not stop the message.
  • Closing the compose window during the countdown cancels the send.
Settings Open the add-on card in Add-ons and Themes and choose its preferences.
  • Delay sending — turn the wait on or off. When it is off, messages are sent immediately.
  • Delay — drag the slider from 5 to 30 seconds. The line under the slider shows the current wait, and the choice is saved as you change it.
